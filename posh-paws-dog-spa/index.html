What’s on the table

Every groom starts with a bath and ends with a finish cut suited to the dog in front of us, not a breed chart.

Bath & Brush

Shampoo suited to coat type, full blow-dry, and brush-out. Good for dogs who just need to look sharp again.

Full Groom

Bath, haircut, and styling to a length that suits the dog and the season, hand-scissored around the face and paws.

Nail Trim & Ears

Nail trim, ear cleaning, and paw pad care, offered on its own or added onto any groom.

Spa Add-Ons

De-shedding treatments, paw balm, and conditioning soaks for dogs with sensitive skin or a heavy coat.

Meet the Owner

One dog on the table means one dog gets my full attention.

Taunya Welch runs Posh Paws by appointment, one dog at a time, so no one is waiting in a crate for their turn. It is slower than a volume shop, and that is the point. Dogs get groomed at their own pace, in a quiet room, by someone who knows their coat.
