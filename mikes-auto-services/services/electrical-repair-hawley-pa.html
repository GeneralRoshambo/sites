Warning Signs of Electrical Problems

  • Battery warning light or check engine light on
  • Car won't start or struggles to start
  • Dimming headlights or interior lights
  • Blown fuses that keep returning
  • Electrical components working intermittently
  • Burning smell from the dash or wiring
  • Battery draining overnight (parasitic draw)

Cold Weather Battery Issues in Hawley, PA

Pennsylvania winters are hard on batteries. Cold temperatures reduce battery capacity significantly — a battery that barely starts your car on a warm day may leave you stranded on a cold January morning near Lake Wallenpaupack or up in the Poconos. If your battery is 3+ years old, have it tested before winter. Mike's tests batteries free of charge during any service visit.

Electrical Repair FAQ

How do I know if it's the battery or the alternator?

If your car won't start but jumps fine and runs, your battery is likely bad. If it dies again shortly after jumping, your alternator isn't charging it. Mike's tests both systems accurately — call (570) 226-6040.

My check engine light is on — is that electrical?

The check engine light can indicate dozens of different issues, from a sensor fault to a serious engine problem. We'll scan the codes, explain what they mean, and give you an honest estimate for the fix.

My battery keeps dying — what's causing it?

A battery draining overnight is called a parasitic draw. Something is pulling power when the car is off. Common culprits are a faulty relay, an interior light, or a failing alternator. We can trace and fix the draw.

How long does a battery last in PA's climate?

Typically 3–5 years, but cold winters accelerate battery degradation. We recommend testing any battery over 3 years old every fall before winter weather hits.
