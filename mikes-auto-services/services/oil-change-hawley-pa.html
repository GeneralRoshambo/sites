How Often Should I Change My Oil?

Modern vehicles with synthetic oil typically go 5,000–7,500 miles between changes, with some manufacturer recommendations up to 10,000 miles. Conventional oil vehicles typically need a change every 3,000–5,000 miles. The Pocono Mountains climate — cold winters and dusty summer roads — can also affect how quickly your oil degrades.

Oil Change FAQ

Conventional vs. synthetic — which is right for me?

Synthetic oil offers better protection at temperature extremes, lasts longer, and is recommended for most newer vehicles. We'll check your owner's manual and recommend the right option for your car and budget.

How long does an oil change take?

Typically 20–30 minutes. If we find other issues during the multi-point inspection that you'd like addressed, we'll give you a separate estimate — no pressure.

What happens if I skip oil changes?

Old, dirty oil loses its lubricating properties, causing excessive engine wear, sludge buildup, and eventually engine failure. Oil changes are the cheapest insurance for your engine.
