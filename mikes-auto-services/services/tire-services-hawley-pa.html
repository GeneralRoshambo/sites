Tire Services for Hawley, PA Drivers

Tires affect everything — stopping distance, fuel economy, handling, and safety. The winding roads through Pike County, the Pocono Mountains, and around Lake Wallenpaupack demand tires that are properly inflated, balanced, and have sufficient tread depth. At Mike's Auto Services & Towing, we provide complete tire care for all makes and models.

Tire Services We Offer

  • Tire rotation (every 5,000–7,500 miles recommended)
  • Tire balancing
  • Flat tire repair and patching
  • Tire pressure check and inflation
  • Tire tread depth inspection
  • Wheel alignment check and service
  • TPMS (Tire Pressure Monitoring System) service
  • Seasonal tire changeover (summer/winter)
  • Tire mounting and installation

Signs Your Tires Need Attention

  • TPMS warning light on dashboard
  • Tread wear indicator bars becoming visible
  • Uneven wear across the tire surface
  • Vibration in steering wheel at highway speeds
  • Vehicle pulling to one side
  • Visible sidewall cracking or bulging
  • Slow air leak / frequently low pressure

Tire Safety in the Pocono Mountains

Pennsylvania winters bring ice and snow, and summer brings rain-slicked mountain curves. We recommend Pennsylvania drivers check their tire tread depth every fall and consider switching to all-season or dedicated winter tires if you travel frequently on Route 6, 507, or other Pocono-area roads between November and March. Tires with less than 4/32" of tread depth in winter conditions significantly increase stopping distances.

Tire Services FAQ

How often should I rotate my tires?

Every 5,000–7,500 miles, or every other oil change. Regular rotation ensures even tread wear across all four tires and significantly extends tire life.

Can a flat tire always be repaired, or do I need a new one?

Most punctures in the tread area can be repaired. Sidewall damage, large tears, or damage caused by running flat cannot — those tires need replacement. We'll tell you honestly which is the case.

What causes my steering wheel to vibrate?

Vibration at highway speeds usually indicates an out-of-balance tire. Vibration at all speeds, especially when turning, may be a suspension issue. Bring it to Mike's and we'll diagnose it correctly.

Do I need winter tires in the Poconos?

All-season tires handle light snow adequately. For frequent winter driving on mountain roads around Hawley and Lake Wallenpaupack, dedicated winter tires provide significantly better traction and shorter stopping distances.
