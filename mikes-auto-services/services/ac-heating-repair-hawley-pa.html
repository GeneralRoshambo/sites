Signs Your AC or Heat Needs Repair

  • AC blowing warm air or not cooling properly
  • Weak airflow from vents
  • Strange odors when AC or heat is on
  • Heater blowing cold air
  • Fogging or dampness inside the vehicle (heater core leak)
  • Clicking or grinding noise from the HVAC system
  • Defroster not clearing windows

FAQ

AC & Heating FAQ

Why is my AC blowing warm air?

The most common causes are low refrigerant (from a leak), a failing compressor, or a clogged condenser. We'll diagnose exactly what's wrong before recommending any repair.

What is a heater core and why does it matter?

The heater core is a small radiator-like component that uses engine coolant to heat your cabin air. When it leaks, you may notice a sweet smell, foggy windows, or wetness on the floor — all signs to address immediately.

How often should I recharge my AC?

AC systems don't normally need regular recharging — if yours is low on refrigerant, there's a leak that needs to be fixed. We locate and repair the leak first, then recharge the system properly.

My defroster barely works in winter — can you fix it?

Yes. Rear defroster issues are often a broken grid element or failed relay. Front defrost problems may be heater-core related. Both are fixable — call (570) 226-6040 to schedule.
