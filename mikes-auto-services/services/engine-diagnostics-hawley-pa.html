Expert Engine Diagnostics in Hawley, Pennsylvania

A lit check engine light can mean anything from a loose gas cap to a misfiring cylinder or a failing catalytic converter. At Mike's Auto Services & Towing in Hawley, PA, we use professional OBD-II diagnostic scanners and advanced tools to read exactly what your vehicle's computer is telling us — then explain it in plain language.

We work on all domestic and import vehicles. We don't guess — we diagnose, then repair.

What We Diagnose & Repair

  • Check engine light diagnosis (OBD-II fault codes)
  • Engine misfires, rough idle, and stalling
  • Oxygen sensor and catalytic converter problems
  • Fuel system issues — injectors, fuel pump, regulators
  • Timing chain and timing belt failures
  • Overheating and coolant system problems
  • Head gasket failures and oil leaks
  • EGR valve and emissions system problems
  • Engine knocking, ticking, or unusual noises

Warning Signs Your Engine Needs Attention

  • Check engine light on (steady or flashing)
  • Rough idle or shaking at startup
  • Loss of power or sluggish acceleration
  • Increased fuel consumption
  • Knocking, ticking, or rattling sounds
  • White, blue, or black smoke from the exhaust
  • Overheating temperature gauge

Our Diagnostic Process

  • Connect professional scanner — read all fault codes
  • Visual inspection alongside electronic diagnosis
  • Explain findings in plain English — no jargon
  • Written estimate before any repair begins
  • You approve — we never start without your go-ahead
  • After repair, codes cleared and issue verified resolved

Engine Diagnostics FAQ

How long does a diagnostic take?

Most scans take 30–60 minutes. If further inspection is needed to pinpoint a specific issue, it may take a bit longer. We'll give you a time estimate upfront.

Is it safe to drive with the check engine light on?

A steady light usually means you can drive carefully to our shop. A flashing check engine light means pull over immediately — it indicates a serious misfire that can damage your catalytic converter.

Do you work on all makes and models?

Yes — we service domestic and import vehicles including Ford, Chevy, GMC, Toyota, Honda, Subaru, Jeep, Dodge, Nissan, and more.

What if my car can't be driven in?

No problem — Mike's offers towing throughout the Hawley area. We'll tow your vehicle to our shop and diagnose it here.
