Scheduled Vehicle Maintenance in Hawley, Pennsylvania

Your vehicle is one of your biggest investments. Keeping up with scheduled maintenance is the most effective way to extend its life, avoid unexpected breakdowns, and maintain its resale value. At Mike's Auto Services & Towing in Hawley, PA, we follow manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedules and give you a clear picture of what your vehicle needs now versus what can wait.

Preventative Maintenance Services

  • Full multi-point vehicle inspection
  • Spark plug inspection and replacement (tune-up)
  • Air filter and cabin air filter replacement
  • Timing belt / timing chain inspection and replacement
  • Serpentine belt and drive belt inspection and replacement
  • Coolant system flush and pressure test
  • Fuel system cleaning and injector service
  • Battery testing and terminal cleaning
  • Wiper blade replacement
  • Pre-purchase vehicle inspection
  • Pre-winter and pre-summer vehicle preparation

Key Maintenance Intervals

  • Every 3,000–5,000 mi: Oil change (conventional) + tire rotation
  • Every 5,000–7,500 mi: Oil change (synthetic) + inspection
  • Every 15,000–30,000 mi: Air filter, cabin filter, fuel filter
  • Every 30,000–60,000 mi: Transmission fluid, coolant flush, spark plugs
  • Every 60,000–100,000 mi: Timing belt, brake fluid, serpentine belt

Protecting Your Vehicle in PA's Climate

The Pocono Mountains region brings cold winters, road salt, and pothole-filled spring roads — all of which accelerate wear. Mike's recommends a pre-winter checkup every fall to ensure your battery, cooling system, tires, and brakes are ready for Pennsylvania's conditions, and a spring inspection to catch any salt or winter damage early.

Maintenance FAQ

How do I know what maintenance my car needs?

Your owner's manual lists the manufacturer's recommended schedule. If you've lost it, bring your vehicle to Mike's — we'll look up your vehicle's service schedule and walk you through what's due based on your mileage.

When should a timing belt be replaced?

Most timing belts are recommended for replacement between 60,000 and 100,000 miles. This is critical — a snapped timing belt can cause catastrophic engine damage. If you don't know when yours was last replaced, have it inspected.

Do you offer pre-winter inspections?

Yes — we strongly recommend a pre-winter checkup every fall. We check your battery, antifreeze level, tires, brakes, heater, and defroster to make sure you're ready for Pennsylvania winters.

Can you inspect a used car before I buy it?

Absolutely. A pre-purchase inspection is one of the best investments you can make. Call us at (570) 226-6040 to schedule — we'll give you a full written report on the vehicle's condition.
