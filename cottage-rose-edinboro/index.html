Our Story

From dry cleaner to found-treasure shop

Gina Mussett spent years in corporate retail management with Express, Limited Express, The Bombay Company, and Pier 1 before she closed on a vacant storefront on Meadville Street the same day she sold her home. What had been a dry cleaner's cement block building became The Cottage Rose, with a restored tin ceiling and exposed brick giving the space its warmth.

Edinboro is both a university town and a lake vacation stop, so the shop is stocked for all three: students furnishing a first apartment, visitors looking for a gift to take home, and neighbors who stop in every week to see what is new.

Something for everyone who walks through the door, not just the vintage collectors. Gina Mussett, owner
