About the Shop

Nearly Forty Years
on 13th Avenue

Trudy Wisner has been washing, clipping, and de-matting the dogs and cats of Dyker Heights and Bensonhurst since 1987. Paws R' Us is the kind of shop where a nail trim does not need an appointment and a nervous puppy's first haircut gets a little extra patience. No chain, no franchise, just one storefront between 76th and 77th Street that neighbors have trusted for close to four decades.
