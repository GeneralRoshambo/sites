Main Street, River Town

Susquehanna Scoops and More sits on Main Street in a small river town that the Susquehanna curls almost all the way around. Locals stop in after Little League games and before the fireworks, for a scoop or a sandwich that doesn't need a chain to taste right.

Year-round ice cream, Boar's Head subs and sandwiches, and the kind of counter service where they know your order by the second visit.

On the Menu

Hard & Soft Serve

Hershey's ice cream, hand-dipped or twisted, in cups, cones and kiddie sizes.

Sundaes & Shakes

The waffle sundae and hand-spun milkshakes made to order at the counter.

Boar's Head Subs

Cold cut subs and sandwiches built fresh, year-round, no ice cream required.
