How it works

One chair, kept for the people who know about it.

NY Barber Co. isn't set up like a walk-in shop. Edmond takes clients by appointment, largely through referral, keeping the schedule tight enough that every cut gets full attention.

It's a deliberate choice: fewer chairs, fewer walk-ins, and a standard that regulars pass along quietly rather than advertise.
