A father-son stand since 2012

Gary Garnic and his son Kang opened The Freezerie in 2012, building a seasonal stand around homemade ice cream made from scratch, batch by batch.

It's stayed a family operation ever since, with new flavors rotating in alongside the favorites people keep coming back for.
