Our Story

Three generations, one counter

The shop has changed hands within the same working family since Samuel Brenman opened its doors, and the name over the register never came down.

1927

Samuel Brenman opens the shop

A neighborhood meat market on Gerritsen Avenue, built on full-service butchering and a made-to-order counter.

Following Decades

Basil carries the trade forward

The shop stays a working butcher counter through the mid-century growth of Gerritsen Beach and Sheepshead Bay.

Next Generation

Edward Izzo takes over

Edward learns the trade from the ground up and keeps the same standards: hand-selected meat, cut on request.

Today

Glenn Izzo runs the counter

Edward's son Glenn started at the shop at age twelve. He now hand-picks every piece of meat that crosses the case, with over three decades behind the counter.
