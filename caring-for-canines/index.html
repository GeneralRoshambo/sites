A real home, not a kennel

Caring for Canines is a one-woman, home-based boarding and pet-sitting service in Greeley. Chrissy has been doing this full time for more than seven years, and set aside half of her own duplex as dedicated space for the dogs staying with her, not a converted garage or a row of kennel runs.

Most clients are local homeowners and second-home owners along the upper Delaware who need someone trustworthy to watch a dog while they travel, without shipping their pet off to a commercial kennel.
