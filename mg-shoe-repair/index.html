About the shop

A corner shop on 9th Avenue

M&G Shoe Repair & Alterations has been the walk-in stop for Chelsea and Hell's Kitchen commuters who need a heel fixed, a zipper replaced, or a hem taken in without shipping a pair off somewhere and waiting a week.

The shop runs on the same idea most old-school repair shops do: look at the shoe, tell you what it actually needs, and fix it while you go run the rest of your errands. No mail-in forms, no guessing at a price online, just a counter and a bench.

Regulars know Marcelo and the team for straight answers on whether a pair is worth resoling or ready for retirement, and for keeping decades-old dress shoes and work boots in rotation.
