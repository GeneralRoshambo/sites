Our Story

A neighborhood pizzeria with a bistro's heart

Tucked into Hamlin Shopping Plaza, Slices Pizzeria & Bistro brings together square-cut Pennsylvania-style pies, hand-stretched New York rounds, and a full bistro menu of subs, burgers, wings, and salads. Everything is made fresh to order, right down to the dough.

Our Hotel Original Red Pizza carries on a recipe served for over 150 years, sitting right alongside modern favorites like our Philly Cheesesteak and Chicago-Style Deep Dish. There is something for every table.
