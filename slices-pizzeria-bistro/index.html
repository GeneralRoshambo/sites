Our Story

A neighborhood pizzeria with a bistro's heart

Tucked into Hamlin Shopping Plaza, Slices Pizzeria & Bistro brings together square-cut Pennsylvania-style pies, hand-stretched New York rounds, and a full bistro menu of subs, burgers, wings, and salads. Everything is made fresh to order, right down to the dough.

Our Hotel Original Red Pizza carries on a recipe served for over 150 years, and it sits right alongside modern favorites like our Philly Cheesesteak and Chicago-Style Deep Dish. There is something for every table.

Open seven days a week for dine-in, takeout, and delivery across Hamlin, we are proud to be a regular stop for families around the lake.
