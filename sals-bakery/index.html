The Bakery

A case that stays full, and a counter that knows your order

Sal's Bakery has built its name on Richmond Valley Road the old fashioned way, a clean, well-stocked case and a staff that remembers regulars by their usual box. Donuts and coffee cake fill the morning rush, while the back counter works through custom orders for birthday cakes, bridal showers, and family gatherings across the South Shore.

Customers keep coming back for the same reasons: fresh bread baked on site, a full pastry case with no gaps by mid-morning, and a cake team that will talk through flavors and design in person before anything gets an order slip. It is the kind of bakery neighbors send visiting family to on purpose.
