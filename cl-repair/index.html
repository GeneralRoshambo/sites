One guy, one shop, everything with an engine

CL Repair is a one-man operation run by Craig Gehrig out of a working shop on Hancock Highway in Equinunk. If it has a small engine, four wheels, or a hitch, there's a good chance it comes through this shop, riding mowers, ATVs, tractors, and lawn equipment for homeowners and farms across the Equinunk and Hancock border area.

Word gets around locally for quick turnaround, especially on mower repairs during the summer when everyone needs their equipment back the same week.
