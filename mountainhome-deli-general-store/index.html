A true general store

Homemade, and known for it

The BLTs are the thing regulars talk about, but the soup rotation and fresh pastry case keep people coming back through the week. It has the feel of an old farmhouse because it is one, tucked at the corner of Route 390 and Laurel Pine Road in Barrett Township.

Locals and Pocono visitors alike stop in for lunch, a coffee, or a loaf of something fresh out of the case, and the shop has built a loyal following almost entirely by word of mouth.

  • Homemade soups, changing daily
  • Stacked deli sandwiches, BLT included
  • Fresh baked pastries and bread
  • Cash only, plan ahead
