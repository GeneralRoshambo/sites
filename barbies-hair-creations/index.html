About the salon

A steady chair on Route 6

Barbie's Hair Creations has been part of the Jermyn business strip for nearly two decades, the kind of place where regulars book their color three weeks out and new clients get greeted by name their second visit. It is a small, personal shop, not a chain, and that shows in the attention given to every cut.

Located right on Route 6, the salon is an easy stop for anyone coming from Mayfield, Archbald or Carbondale, with flexible scheduling for both quick trims and longer color services.
