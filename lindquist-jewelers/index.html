A rare craft, still practiced here

Arne Lindquist is a graduate watchmaker, certified since 1969, one of a shrinking number of jewelers who can still repair a watch movement on-site rather than sending it out.

It's a specialty that's become increasingly rare, and it's been part of Lindquist Jewelers since the doors opened in 1985.

Since 1985, Arne Lindquist has run his namesake jewelry store with a focus on fine jewelry, diamonds, and watches, backed by a watchmaking certification dating back to 1969.
