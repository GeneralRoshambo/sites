Grown here, by someone from here

  • OwnerDeborah Young
  • NeighborhoodBed-Stuy, Brooklyn
  • Open since2012
  • Address358 Stuyvesant Ave

Deborah Young has been growing plants since she was three years old. She grew up in Bed-Stuy, studied marine biology, and ran a floral design business in the 1980s before opening Seasons in 2012 on a lot that had been left to go to weeds.

She turned it into a green corner where neighbors stop in, ask questions and leave with something that will actually grow where they live. More than a shop, it is a place for community and connection, with flowers, art and decorations changing as the year turns.
