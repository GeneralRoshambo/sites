About

A Counter the Neighborhood Grew Up On

Family run, Bronx owned, open every day of the week.

National Bakery has been part of the Intervale Avenue block for years, the kind of shop where the line moves slow because everyone stops to talk. Neighbors come in for a small cup of oatmeal before work, a Cubano at lunch, or a full sheet cake for a christening the same week.

Everything in the case is baked on site. The tres leches is the one people drive across the Bronx for, soaked heavy and finished with a real dulce de leche, not a syrup. Regulars order it by the box for holidays, and Uber Eats and DoorDash keep it moving to those who cannot make it in.
