What's in the case

The counter changes with the day and the season, but these are the ones regulars come in asking for by name.

  • CannoliFilled to order

    Crisp shells piped with sweet ricotta cream when you ask, so the shell stays crunchy. Cannoli horns too.

  • Rainbow cookiesBy the pound

    Almond sponge in green, white and red, layered with jam and finished in dark chocolate.

  • Lobster tailsPastry classic

    Flaky sfogliatella shells stuffed with cream.

  • DonutsFresh daily

    Glazed, filled and specialty donuts, including the PB&J donut people drive over for.

  • Cheesecake conesHouse favorite

    Cheesecake filling piped into a crisp cone. Easy to eat on the walk back to the car.

  • Cookies, buns and breadsTrays available

    Italian butter cookies, sweet buns and bread for the table, boxed and tied for holidays and visits.

Custom cakes for every occasion

Birthdays, communions, showers and anniversaries. Bring a photo, a theme or just a flavor and the team will design a cake around it, from classic cannoli cream cakes to an espresso martini cake.

  • Call or stop in with your date, size and design idea
  • Pick your cake, filling and finish
  • Pick it up boxed and ready at 821 Annadale Road
