About Old Ranger's Inn

A Diamond in the Rough, Deep in the Woods

Set along Route 390 in the forests near Promised Land State Park, Old Ranger's Inn has become a go-to stop for locals and Pocono visitors alike. Behind the kitchen, Chef Pete and the crew turn out generous, made-to-order comfort food — from smoked ribs to a proper Delmonico steak — without any pretense.

Pair your meal with a drink from the full bar, settle in at a table, and enjoy the kind of unhurried, welcoming service that keeps people coming back trip after trip to the Poconos.

  • Full bar & friendly, attentive service
  • Hearty, scratch-made tavern fare
  • Minutes from Promised Land State Park & Lake Wallenpaupack
  • Takeout available — reservations welcome
