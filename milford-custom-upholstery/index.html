A small farm upbringing, a lifetime with a needle

"Debi is available to help customers choose fabrics and finishes to make their pieces perfect."

Debi De Giorgis was born and raised on a small farm in Orange County, NY, and has worked at just about every craft since, ceramics, cake decorating, and above all textiles and home furnishings. She has sewn since childhood, starting on her mother's antique treadle machines.

Milford Custom Upholstery is a one-woman workroom based in Shohola Falls, serving clients throughout Milford, Matamoras, Dingmans Ferry, and the rest of Pike County by appointment. Every project starts with a conversation about how a piece is used, not just how it looks.

Debi De Giorgis Owner, Milford Custom Upholstery
