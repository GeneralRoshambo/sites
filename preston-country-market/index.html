Our Story

A true family general store

Preston Country Market has been the place folks in Starrucca and Preston Park stop for a fresh sandwich, a full grocery run, or a tank of gas, all without leaving Shehawken Road. We are family owned and operated, and it shows in the deli case and on the shelves.

Our kitchen turns out hand-made sandwiches, salads, and stromboli every day, while our shelves stay stocked with groceries, produce, and hardware for whatever the week brings.

Whether you are a neighbor or just passing through Wayne County, we aim to make the stop worth it.
