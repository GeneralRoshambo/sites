A Girard Township staple

Where the regulars come daily

Girard Cafe, known to most as Ralphie's, has been dishing out breakfast and lunch to the same Girard Township families for generations. It is not a place chasing trends, it is the diner that opens early, serves fast, and treats every regular like family.

Retirees and working locals fill the counter through the morning, and the lunch crowd keeps the kitchen just as busy with sandwiches and daily soup specials.
