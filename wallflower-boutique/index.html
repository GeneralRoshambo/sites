Our Story

Run by Joyce and Dean, on the same block, for forty years.

Joyce and Dean DeBastiani opened Wallflower on Main Street in 1985, and they're still the ones behind the counter. What started as a small clothing shop has grown into a 4,000 square foot destination across two floors, built one season of buying trips and one regular customer at a time.

Their approach hasn't changed: mix clothing with jewelry, candles, and gifts, keep the selection moving, and know most people who walk in by name. It's the kind of shop where you come in for a birthday card and leave with a new sweater too.
