How a custom order works

Three simple steps from first message to party table.

  1. Send the details

    Email or DM your date, what you are celebrating, how many people you are feeding and any photos or colors you love.

  2. Settle the design

    We reply with ideas, flavors and a price, and adjust until the design is exactly right.

  3. Pick up and celebrate

    Your order is baked fresh for the day and ready for pickup, or delivery can be arranged in the tri-state area.
