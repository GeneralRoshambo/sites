PROVENANCE

Sixteen years on Harford Street

Shaun has been tattooing since 1999 and opened Golden Hammer in Milford in 2009. What started as a tattoo studio grew a second life as a gathering point for the town's antiques, jewelry, and things nobody else knew what to do with.

Golden Hammer is a working tattoo and piercing studio first: custom pieces, cover-ups, fine line, black and grey, and color, built around a conversation rather than a flash wall. Five artists and two piercers cover most styles a client walks in asking for, and every session starts with a sit-down design consult, not a rush job off a printed sheet.

Past the front counter, the shop doubles as a curated stop for local antiques, estate jewelry, candles, and the kind of odd, well-made objects that Milford's older buildings tend to turn up. Regulars come in for a piece of jewelry and leave having booked a tattoo, or the other way around.

FRONT ROOM

The front room

Not a gift-shop afterthought. Half the building is a working curiosity shop.

Between clients, Shaun and the artists keep the front room stocked with finds from estate sales and swaps around Pike County. It changes shop to shop, week to week, which is the point.

  • Estate & vintage jewelry
  • Small antiques & oddities
  • Hand-poured candles
  • Local art & prints
  • Trade & consignment pieces
  • New finds weekly
