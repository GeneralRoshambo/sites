A neighborhood counter, not a chain.

Chrissie's has been the go-to stop for folks in Matamoras who want a sandwich made right, not assembled off a screen. It's a small, cash-only operation on Pennsylvania Ave, the kind of place where the person behind the counter remembers your order.

Open six days a week starting at 7am, it's equal parts breakfast spot and lunch counter, serving the Upper Delaware River corner of Pike County one sub at a time.
