Exterior Wash and Protection in Eureka, MO

A hand wash and protective finish that keeps the shine longer between visits.

Automatic washes are quick, but the brushes that clean hundreds of cars a day are not gentle on paint. Our exterior service is a two-bucket hand wash, done one vehicle at a time, so we can actually see what we are working on.

We clean the wheels and tires separately from the body to avoid cross-contamination, hand dry to prevent water spots, and finish with a protective sealant that helps the paint shed dirt, water, and road film.

The result is a finish that looks better on day one and stays cleaner longer, whether it sits in a driveway in Eureka or gets driven every day.
