Stain and Odor Removal in Eureka, MO

Focused treatment for the spots and smells a regular clean will not fix.

Some messes need more than a vacuum. Spilled drinks, pet accidents, and ground-in dirt sit deep in the carpet and seat fibers, and smells like smoke or pet odor come from the padding underneath, not just the surface.

We identify the type of stain or odor first, then use the right extraction method and treatment rather than a one-size-fits-all spray. That means actually lifting the stain and neutralizing the source of the smell instead of masking it.

This service pairs well with an interior deep clean, or stands on its own if you just need one specific problem solved before a trip or a sale.
