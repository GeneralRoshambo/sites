About Caldentey Cleaning Co.

A local, owner-run house cleaning service built on trust and attention to detail.

Owner & operator

Enrique Caldentey

Caldentey Cleaning Co. is run by Enrique Caldentey, who started the business in Jacksonville to bring reliable, honest, and detail-oriented cleaning to local homes. Every client is a chance to earn trust and build the kind of reputation that grows on referrals.

The approach is simple. Show up when promised, follow a consistent room-by-room checklist, and treat each home with the same care as if it were our own. That means the spots other cleaners rush past get the attention they deserve.

Being local and owner-run means you always know who is caring for your home, and you are supporting a small Jacksonville business that genuinely appreciates the work.
