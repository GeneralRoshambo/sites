About Pennington Countertop Restoration in Volusia County, FL

A new countertop restoration business built on a real decorative concrete coating background.

Based in Volusia County, Florida

Run by Katie Pennington

Pennington Countertop Restoration is run by Katie Pennington, who trained at a dedicated decorative concrete coatings school before taking on her first custom countertop build. Every piece is built and finished by hand, from the initial prep work through the final sealed coat.

The business is brand new, which means early customers get close attention on every job and pricing built around establishing a strong local portfolio, not padded for a big operation.

Katie is based in Volusia County and takes jobs across the surrounding area, from resurfacing an existing countertop in place to building a custom piece from scratch.
