Mobile Diagnostics in Dallas, TX

A check engine light or rough idle, actually diagnosed instead of guessed at.

A check engine light can mean a dozen different things, and guessing wrong wastes money on parts that were never the problem. We bring a professional-grade scan tool to your location and pull the actual codes, then walk the vehicle down to confirm what is really going on.

That means real answers on the spot: whether it is a sensor, a vacuum leak, an ignition issue, or something more involved, you get a clear explanation and a straightforward quote before any repair starts.

This service also covers drivability complaints that do not throw a code at all, rough idle, hesitation, or a noise that only shows up sometimes.
