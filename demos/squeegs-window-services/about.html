About Squeegs Window Services in Santa Ana, CA

A window and solar cleaning company built on doing every pane by hand.

Based in Santa Ana, California

Started with a squeegee and a route

Squeegs Window Services began in Santa Ana cleaning residential windows one house at a time, and has grown into a full window and solar panel cleaning operation serving homes and businesses across Orange County.

Every job is done by hand with a traditional squeegee rather than a spray-and-wipe approach, because that is what actually gets glass streak-free instead of just wet and dry again.

Solar panel cleaning rounds out the work, since dust and grime buildup on panels quietly cuts into how much power a system actually produces.
