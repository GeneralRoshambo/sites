About the Area

Where to Go and What to See

Both Hawley and Milford are quaint Victorian-era towns with great art galleries, antiquing, and fine foods.

Nearby

Waterfalls, trails, and small-town charm

The area also has scenic waterfalls, horseback riding, hiking trails, scenic state parks, and recreation & picnic areas.

Claws & Paws Petting Zoo

A family-favorite petting zoo, just a short drive from the cottages.

Golf & Mini-Golf

Tournament-quality golf courses, plus mini-golf and go-carts for the kids.

Dining for Every Taste

Restaurants of all cuisines, from fast food to 4-star fine dining, plus convenient liquor and supermarkets.

A Local Favorite

The Hawley Antique Exchange

Lake Wallenpaupack’s largest multi-dealer shop. For 30 years, our dealers have worked hard to fill 8,000 square feet of shop space, bringing a wide selection of art, antiques, and collectibles — always buying, single items or entire estates.

www.haexchange.com →

Not to Miss

The Martin Collection

Located on the lower floor of the Hawley Antique Exchange — home to the world’s largest collection of Vaseline/Uranium glass.

World’s Largest Collection of Vaseline/Uranium Glass

Including 100-year-old vaseline glass, all glowing under hundreds of ultra-violet lights. In addition, the collection includes rare music boxes, clocks, and a Steinway Grand reproducing piano playing Chopin, Mozart, Gershwin, and more.

www.vaselineglasstosee.com →
