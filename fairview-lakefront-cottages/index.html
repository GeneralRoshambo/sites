A Fisherman’s Dream

200 acres, stocked and thriving

Fairview Lake is a natural, deep, spring-fed lake stocked with Brown, Rainbow, and Palomino trout — once written up in Outdoor Life as one of the Poconos’ best lakes for both small- and large-mouth bass.

  • Aluminum boat with 6HP motor included with every cottage
  • Hard-sand swimming area by the main dock, safe for kids
  • Bass, Perch, Pickerel, Catfish, Eel, and plenty of blue gill
