ABOUT THE BAKERY

Family run, counter to case

Bakery Boys of New York is a family owned bakery on 104th Street, baking Italian bread and cookies for the Corona neighborhood every morning. The ovens run early, and the counter stays stocked through the evening for whoever walks in for a loaf, a coffee, or a box of cookies to bring somewhere.

The same kitchen supplies restaurants, delis and caterers around Queens by the case, so whether you are picking up a single Italian loaf or ordering rolls for a full event, it comes out of the same ovens.
