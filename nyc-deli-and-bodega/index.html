The Corner Stop Bay Ridge Runs On

NYC Deli & Bodega holds down the corner of 3rd Avenue from early morning until close to midnight, every day of the week. It is the kind of stop that covers a lot of ground: a bacon, egg and cheese on the way to the train, a sandwich stacked to order at lunch, milk or paper towels when the store run cannot wait, and a coffee whenever the block needs one.

Behind the counter it runs the way a good neighborhood deli should, quick, familiar, and open long past when most other options have closed for the night.
