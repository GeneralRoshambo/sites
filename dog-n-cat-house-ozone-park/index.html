A neighborhood fixture since 1980

Christina DiToro has run The Dog 'n Cat House on Crossbay Boulevard for over four decades, taking in everything from nervous first-time puppies to cats who'd rather not be there at all. It's the kind of shop where the regulars don't need to give their pet's name at drop-off.

No franchise polish, no rush. Just a full-service, all-breed grooming salon that's outlasted three generations of Ozone Park's other pet businesses, and still does the job by hand.
