Our Story

Same building, same purpose, since 1888

Long before highways and chain stores reached Wayne County, Poyntelle General Store was already open, selling what the town needed and holding onto the things worth keeping. That hasn't changed.

Today it's still the one stop for a horse halter, a pound of fudge, and a birthday gift, all under one roof, all from people who know the difference between passing through and staying put.
