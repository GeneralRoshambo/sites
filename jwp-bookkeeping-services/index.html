One bookkeeper. One inbox. One person who actually knows your business when you call.

JWP Bookkeeping Services LLC has kept the books for independent contractors and small businesses around Washington Heights and Inwood since 2020. No account managers, no rotating staff, no software you have to learn on your own. Jim reconciles the accounts himself, categorizes every transaction by hand, and flags anything that looks off before it becomes a problem at tax time.

Work is done bilingually in English and Spanish, which matters in a neighborhood built on family-run shops, contractors, and independent tradespeople who need someone who understands both the language and the paperwork.
