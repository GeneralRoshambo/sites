A Fixture on New Dorp Lane

New Dorp Deli sits in the middle of one of Staten Island's busiest little strips, steps from the Staten Island Railway and a short ride from New Dorp Beach. It is the kind of counter where the person making your sandwich already knows what you want before you say it.

The deli has never needed a website to fill the counter, foot traffic and word of mouth have done that job for years. That also means most people who search for a New Dorp deli online never see this one show up.
