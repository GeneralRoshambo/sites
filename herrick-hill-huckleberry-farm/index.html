A family field, open to everyone in picking season

Herrick Hill Huckleberry Farm is a small, family-run blueberry patch in Herrick Center, tucked into the hills above Herrick Township. Come mid-July, the bushes fill in and the fields open for u-pick, drawing families from Wayne and Susquehanna counties looking for an afternoon outdoors and a full bucket to bring home.

There's no shop, no packaging line, just rows of bushes and a scale at the stand. Picking is weather-dependent, so the fastest way to know if the fields are open on a given day is a quick call or a look at our Facebook page before you drive out.

What to expect in the field

Three things worth knowing before you make the drive out to Airport Drive.

Ripe by the row

Bushes ripen at slightly different times across the field. We can point you toward the fullest rows when you arrive.

Bring a container

A bucket, a basket, whatever holds a few pounds. We weigh and price at the stand when you're done.

Weather calls the day

Wet fields close for the day. Call ahead or check our Facebook page before making the drive.
