A Middletown tailor since 1979

Armando Collarelli opened his tailor shop on N Street in 1979, and it has served Middletown ever since. What began as a custom sewing and alterations shop grew to include Formal Wear by Armando's for weddings and formal occasions.

Every piece that leaves the shop still gets the same hands-on attention it did on day one.
