From Dona-Helene to Legacy.

What began as Dona-Helene Hair & Nail Salon has carried 50 years of history forward as Legacy Salon, now the largest full-service salon in Milford. The name changed, but the commitment to the town didn't.

Today Legacy Salon offers hair, nails, pedicures, waxing, and retail products for men and women, rated 4.9 stars by the community it's served for half a century.
