Liner notes

Handmade, vintage and fair trade, picked with care.

Cosmik Gifts is a locally owned shop in North Scranton built around one idea: the best things already exist. Second-hand clothes, records with a few decades on them and books that have been read before sit right next to work from local makers.

Shoppers describe it as a chill, kind place to browse, and the selection is refreshed often, so the crate you flipped through last month is not the crate you will find today. If you are hunting for a gift nobody else will think of, this is a good place to start.

  • Locally made
  • Vintage and second-hand
  • Fair trade
  • Upcycled
