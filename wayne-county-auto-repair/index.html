About the Shop

A community garage, not a chain

Wayne County Auto Repair sits at the corner of Galilee Road and Route 371 in Damascus Township, just over the bridge from Cochecton, New York. The shop handles the everyday work that keeps a car on the road: state inspections, oil changes, and snow tire swaps, done by people who live in the area.

The waiting room was recently redone with heat, wifi, and coffee, a small thing that says a lot about how the shop treats the wait, not just the work.
