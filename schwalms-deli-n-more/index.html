Schwalm's Deli N More has been the place along Route 92 where neighbors grab lunch and fill in the grocery gaps between bigger trips into town. It's the kind of counter where the person slicing your order probably knows your name.

No frills, no self-checkout, just a deli case, a grocery aisle, and people who've kept it running for over twenty years.
