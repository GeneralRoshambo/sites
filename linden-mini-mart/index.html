A Steady Hand on Linden Street

Mike Slavich has run Linden Mini Mart as a straightforward corner store, the kind of place regulars stop into on the way home without needing an app or a loyalty card. It has kept the block stocked for years on nothing more than a good location and a reliable owner behind the counter.

The store has never had its own website, which means the people searching online for a corner store near Linden Street in Scranton have no way of finding it.
