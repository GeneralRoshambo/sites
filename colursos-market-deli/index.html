A West Scranton Fixture Since 1982

Colurso's opened on Luzerne Street in 1982 and became one of West Scranton's go-to stops for deli meat and fresh produce. When longtime owners Pat and Charlotte Colurso retired, new owners stepped in to keep the counter running and the mom-and-pop feel intact.

It has always run on foot traffic and word of mouth around the neighborhood, which also means most people searching online for a West Scranton deli never find their way to this counter.
