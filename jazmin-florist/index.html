Sobre nosotros

A flower shop that knows the neighborhood by name

Tucked between 174th and 175th Street, Jazmin Florist keeps roses, lilies, carnations, and seasonal greens on hand for whatever the day calls for. Most orders still come in the way they always have, a phone call, a walk-in, or a message on Instagram, with no online ordering system standing between a customer and what they need.

Sympathy and memorial work makes up a steady share of the shop's business, alongside the birthdays, get-well bouquets, and quinceañera and wedding orders that keep the coolers full.

Flowers for every occasion, from first birthdays to last goodbyes

The shop's work breaks down into a handful of real categories that repeat all year, each one tagged the way an order actually leaves the counter. Here is what comes through the door.

Celebraciones

Birthdays, newborns, and get-well wishes

Bright, colorful arrangements for the good news, a new baby, a birthday, a friend on the mend. Walk-in orders are usually ready before you finish parking.

Bodas y aniversarios

Weddings and anniversaries

Roses and seasonal blooms built around a date that matters, from a first anniversary bouquet to a full order for a quinceañera or a wedding party.

En memoria

Sympathy and memorial arrangements

Arreglos fúnebres are some of the most requested work in the shop, standing sprays, casket pieces, and sympathy bouquets put together with the same care whether the order comes a week ahead or the same morning.

Plantas Plants and everyday greenery

Potted plants and simple arrangements for no occasion at all, just something green for a front desk, a kitchen table, or a new apartment.
