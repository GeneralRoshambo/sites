Why We're Here

Khlovan's Thrift opened on East Market Street with a simple goal: give the things Scranton already owns a longer life. Clothing, dishware, books, and furniture come in as donations and go back out at prices built for a household budget, not a boutique markup.

The shop is named for its owners, Kavan Hughes and Chloe Conway, who moved the store from an idea into a storefront in their hometown region and have run it themselves ever since.

"I just always loved finding something that was loved by somebody else before. It felt really nice and fulfilling to find new homes for these things." Kavan Hughes, Co-Owner

How A Donation Becomes Somebody's Find

Every piece on the floor started as something a neighbor didn't need anymore. Kavan and Chloe sort what comes through the door, price it fairly, and put it back into circulation for whoever needs it next.

Chloe handles most of the counter, and the conversations that come with it. "I really like getting to talk to customers and the locals that live around," she says. It's a small shop run by two people who live a few miles up the road in Clarks Summit and built this one on purpose.

  • Donations accepted during all open hours
  • Fitting room available on the lower level
  • New markdowns posted most weekends

Bring Us What You Don't Need

If it's clean, usable, and taking up closet space, we'll find it a home. No appointment required, just bring it by during open hours and hand it across the counter.
