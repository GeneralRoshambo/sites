A Certified Groomer, Not a Corporate Chain

Shannon Ceccoli has spent more than three decades behind the grooming table, earning her grooming certification and a business degree along the way. She grooms every dog and cat herself, by appointment, at her shop on Pittston Avenue, so the groomer who answers the phone is the same one holding the clippers.

When she is not grooming, Shannon serves as president of Northeast PA Pet Fund & Rescue, the local group that helps place and care for animals across Lackawanna County. Neighbors bring her their pets because they trust her, not because she is the closest listing on a map.
