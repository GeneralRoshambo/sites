Our Story

Milanville's social center since 1979

The Milanville General Store and Post Office sits along the idyllic Delaware River in the small enclave of Milanville, untouched by major highways and development. It is the business and social center of the Milanville Historic District, entered on the National Register of Historic Places.

Inside, the deli turns out made to order breakfast, lunch, and Rocco's famous pizza, while baker Vanessa keeps the case stocked with homemade sweets. It is still, after all these years, the kind of store where you can find nearly everything imaginable.
