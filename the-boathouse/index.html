A Water Lovers’ Haven

Dock your boat.
Pull up a chair.

The Boathouse is a true water lovers’ haven — simple, delicious, time-proven classics served in an atmosphere easy enough for flip-flops and dressed-up enough for date night. Whether you’ve driven in on PA-507 or eased up to our dock on Wallenpaupack, there’s a seat waiting on the water.

We’re part of the Pocono Mountain Hospitality family of restaurants, alongside Fairview Cafe and White Pine Inn — three kitchens, one commitment to scratch cooking and local sourcing.

“An atmosphere appropriate for guests in any attire — whether arriving by car or by boat.”
