Four decades of local ground work

Pirog & Garvey Excavating has handled site work and septic installations around Pine Bush since roughly 1986, long enough to have dug the ground under three generations of local homes. The crew stays small on purpose, about three people, which keeps every job under close, direct oversight.

Homeowners and small builders in the Wallkill River valley call for the same reason people have called for forty years: the job gets done right the first time.
