About the Business

Family-run excavation, built on referrals.

Brozonis Excavating is a Union Dale based excavation contractor handling everything from residential driveways to full commercial site prep. The business is registered as a commercial carrier, meaning the trucks, the equipment, and the crew are set up to handle real job sites, not just small backyard digs.

Most work comes from word of mouth across Susquehanna and Wayne County. Homeowners, builders, and township projects call because the last job got done on time and done right.

  • Owner-operated, on every job site
  • Fully licensed and insured commercial carrier
  • Serving Union Dale, Clifford, Herrick, and surrounding townships
  • Residential and commercial project experience
  • Equipment ready for grading, trenching, and demolition
