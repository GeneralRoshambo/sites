Started at a table. Grew into a storefront.

D&D's Collectables began in 2005 as a single table at a flea market in Exeter. Word got around about the finds, and the table grew into a full storefront on North Main Ave, known to regulars simply as The NEPA Pickers.

Owner Dan Kosloski and the team still do the legwork themselves, sourcing estate finds, old collections, and forgotten boxes from across northeast Pennsylvania, then sorting it all out for the next collector to discover.

If you've got a collection to sell, coins to appraise, or an afternoon to spend digging through history, this is the shop for it.

What We Buy & Sell: Vintage Advertising, Old Magazines, and Local Memorabilia

Beyond coins and comics, the shelves carry matchbook collections, vintage newspapers, sports cards, and one-of-a-kind pieces of NEPA history. Looking to sell a collection instead of buy one? Bring it in, or call ahead to set up a time to have it looked over.
