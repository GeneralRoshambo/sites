Freedhome Variety is a hardware and general goods store on Main Street in Bloomingburg, serving the community with the everyday items people would otherwise drive out of town for, hardware, housewares, greeting cards, and seasonal goods.

It's a fixture for local shopping, rated highly by the customers who rely on it, but its only public presence today is a Facebook page and scattered directory listings.
