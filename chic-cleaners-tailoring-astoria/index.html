About the shop

Precision, not guesswork

Chic Cleaners & Tailoring has worked out of the same storefront on 30th Avenue, taking in everything from a torn seam to a full wedding gown fitting. The shop is run day to day by the same couple who measure, pin, and press every garment themselves.

No drop box, no outsourcing. What comes in the door gets worked on right there.

Measured twice, altered once

Every alteration starts with an honest read on fit, not just a quick pin job. That's the difference between clothes that fit for a season and clothes that fit for years.

What we handle

Every garment, properly fitted

From a quick hem to a full dry clean, each job gets checked and pressed before it goes back on the hanger.

Hemming & Alterations

Pants, skirts, and dresses taken in, let out, or shortened to fit exactly how you want.

Dry Cleaning

Suits, coats, and delicates cleaned and pressed, ready on hangers for pickup.

Wedding Dress Fitting

Gowns fitted in person, with enough time built in for a second fitting before the big day.

Suit Tailoring

Jackets and trousers adjusted at the shoulder, waist, and cuff for a fit that reads custom.

Zipper & Repair Work

Broken zippers, torn linings, and loose buttons fixed without a full remake.

Same-Week Turnaround

Most alterations are ready within a few days, with rush options for tighter timelines.

A specialty of the shop

Wedding dress alterations, done right

Gowns take more care than a hem, and the shop treats them that way.

First Fitting

The dress is pinned and marked in person, with the bride there to check the fit while standing.

Alteration Work

Hems, straps, and bodices adjusted by hand, with attention to the fabric and beading involved.

Final Fitting

A second fitting confirms everything sits right before the dress goes home for the wedding.
