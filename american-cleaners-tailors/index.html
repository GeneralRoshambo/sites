  • Fur and leather care alongside standard garments
  • Military patch and uniform work

Run by the family that started it

Vincent Zeppieri Sr. opened the shop's doors in 1964, back when the sign out front still read One Hour Martinizing. His son Vincent Jr. grew up in the back of the store and now runs it the same way his father did: measure twice, press once, and know the customer's name.

That kind of continuity is rare on Tremont Avenue. The shop has stayed in the same family, in the same storefront, through six decades of Throggs Neck.

Vincent Zeppieri, Owner

"They've been pressing my husband's suits for twenty years. Same family, same care, every single time."

Longtime Customer, Throggs Neck
