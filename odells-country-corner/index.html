A Route 209 institution

Gas, groceries, and a deli counter that delivers

The O'Dell family has run this corner for generations, and the deli counter is the reason people pull off Route 209 instead of driving past. Cheesesteaks, hot pastrami, and made-to-order pizza sit alongside the usual gas station shelf of essentials.

It serves two different crowds at once: neighbors who stop in daily and travelers passing between Port Jervis and the Route 17 corridor who just need gas, a sandwich, and directions.
