The salt room

Dry salt therapy, or halotherapy, uses a halogenerator to grind salt into fine particles and release them into the room. People come in for easier breathing, for their skin, or simply for a quiet half hour away from the phone.

Wear loose, comfortable, light colored clothing. If you are coming for a skin condition, leave that area uncovered.
