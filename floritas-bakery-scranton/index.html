A neighborhood panadería

Florita's Bakery sits on Cedar Avenue in South Side Scranton, baking the breads and sweets that fill a Mexican bakery case: pan dulce and pastries, danishes, breads and cookies, made daily.

It works the way a panadería should. Walk in, grab a tray and tongs, choose what looks best, and the counter boxes it up. Stop in for a concha with your morning coffee, or pick up a dozen for the office.
