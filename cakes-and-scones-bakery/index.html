Since 2004

Twenty-five years of pastry work, now on Route 507

Tere spent twenty-five years baking professionally in restaurant kitchens around Boston and New York City before opening Cakes & Scones in Greentown in 2004. She and Andy have run the counter together ever since, turning out scones, danish, eclairs, tiramisu, and Portuguese custards using only butter, fresh ingredients, and Belgian chocolate.

The bakery has become a regular stop for the Lake Wallenpaupack and Greentown crowd, with a small team hand-finishing everything from a Tuesday morning muffin order to a full wedding cake, made from scratch, in house, the same way it has been since day one.

Planning a wedding cake or a party order

Tere designs and bakes every wedding and celebration cake to order, from a simple two tier to a full reception centerpiece. Call ahead with your date, guest count, and flavor preferences, and she will build the order with you over the phone or at the counter.
