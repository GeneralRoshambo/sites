Run by the Block, for the Block

Edward Powers has kept the deli counter stocked and the lights on at West 230th Street for over two decades, through changing blocks and changing seasons. It is a small operation, three people on any given shift, which is exactly why regulars get greeted by name and orders get remembered.

No app, no ordering portal, just a phone that gets answered and a counter that opens early. That has been enough for Kingsbridge for twenty years, but it also means most people searching for a Kingsbridge deli online never find this one.
