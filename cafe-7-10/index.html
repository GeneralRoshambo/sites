Our Story

Built By Mom And Daughter.

Cafe 7-10 is owned and run by Thelma Mae Blanks and her daughter Mabel Howard, a black-owned small business at the corner of 7th and 10th in downtown Erie.

What started as a coffee counter has grown into a gathering spot, pastries in the morning, poetry on Tuesdays, and a standing invitation to pull up a chair.

"We wanted a place where people would actually stay."

Thelma Mae Blanks & Mabel Howard, Owners
