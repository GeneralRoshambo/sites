Our Story

A true country cafe on the way to the lake

Country Cafe & Pizzeria is the kind of spot where regulars have their usual table and visitors heading to Lake Wallenpaupack stop in and become regulars too. We serve breakfast all morning, hearty lunch plates, and dinner favorites, all made from scratch in our own kitchen.

Our homemade soups and mouthwatering burgers keep folks coming back, and our hand-stretched pizza rounds out a menu built for every meal of the day.

Located right on Beach Lake Highway, we are proud to be a family gathering spot for the Beach Lake and Lake Wallenpaupack community, seven days a week.
