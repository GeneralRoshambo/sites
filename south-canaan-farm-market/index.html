Family run, since day one

A market built around the counter, not the aisles.

South Canaan Farm Market opened as a straightforward country market: a real deli counter, farm goods, and staples at fair prices, with a staff that treats regulars like neighbors.

Out-of-state customers have come to depend on mail orders when they can't make the drive, a sign of how much the deli itself, not just the location, keeps people coming back.
