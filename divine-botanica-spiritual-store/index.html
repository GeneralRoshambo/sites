Our Story on Liberty Avenue

Divine Botanica & Spiritual Store has been part of the Liberty Avenue corridor in South Richmond Hill since 2020, stocking the candles, oils, statues and Puja goods that the neighborhood's Guyanese, Indo-Caribbean and South Asian families have always shopped for close to home.

It's a walk-in shop first. Regulars come by for a specific candle or a bath before a big week, and leave with a little more guidance than they came in for.
