Small shop, short hours, worth the trip

Broome Street Bargains is a small community thrift shop on N Broome St. It is run on a short, volunteer schedule, just two mornings a week.

Because the shop keeps limited hours, it helps to plan ahead. Stop by Thursday or Saturday morning to see what has come in.
