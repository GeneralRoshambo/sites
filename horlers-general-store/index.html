Nearly a century on the same corner

Horler's General Store has served the tiny village of Unionville since 1927, when Thomas Horler first opened its doors. It changed hands over the decades, and today it's a working deli and general store where locals stop for sandwiches, bakery goods, coffee, and small grocery items.

The Village of Unionville's own website lists it as one of the town's local businesses, without a link to a website, because there isn't one, just a phone number and a century of word-of-mouth.
