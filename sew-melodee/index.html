About the Studio

Sew Melodee is the dressmaking and alterations studio of Susanne "Melodee" Smith, built on more than twenty-five years of pattern-making, draping, and construction. There is no shop window and no rack of ready-made stock. Every order starts with a conversation, a measurement, and a plan for how the garment needs to move.

Most clients find Melodee the way Park Slope has always found its best tailors and dressmakers: word of mouth, one finished gown or one perfectly re-hemmed pair of trousers at a time. She also teaches, running private sewing lessons for beginners and advanced students, and has taught a fashion design class to local high school students.
