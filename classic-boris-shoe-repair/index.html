A bench, not a factory

Every pair that comes through the door gets looked at by hand before a single stitch is touched. No mail-in service, no assembly line. Just a cobbler who has been fixing shoes for this neighborhood long enough to know most of his customers by their gait.

About the shop

Old-world repair work, still done the old way

Classic Boris Shoe Repair sits on 181st Street, where it has kept Washington Heights in working shoes and repaired handbags for years. The tools are the same ones a cobbler would recognize from decades ago: a stitching horse, a last, a heel iron warmed on the burner.

  • Hand resoling with genuine leather and rubber options
  • Heel replacement while you wait, in most cases
  • Zipper, strap, and lining repair for bags and boots
  • Stretching for tight or narrow-fitting shoes
