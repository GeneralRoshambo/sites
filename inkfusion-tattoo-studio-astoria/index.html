The Studio

Run by artists who work here every day

Inkfusion has been on Steinway Street putting together custom pieces and clean piercing work for the Astoria neighborhood. Giovanni leads on black and gray realism and larger custom work, while Kiara handles piercing with the kind of patience that makes a first piercing easy.

The shop keeps its floor clean and organized and takes walk-ins every day it's open, alongside free consultations for anyone still working out placement, size, or style before committing to a piece.

On The Sheet

What the shop works in
