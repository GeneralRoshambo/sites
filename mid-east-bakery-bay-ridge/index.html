Our story

Three generations, one oven

Mid-East Bakery and Grocery has been a fixture of 3rd Avenue since 1976, when Antoine and Frangie Tabet first opened its doors. Their daughter Marie now runs the shop with her husband Tony Aflak, and their sons Alex and Thomas have grown up behind the same counter.

The bread has not changed. Neither has the welcome you get walking through the door.

Run by the Aflak family

Tony and Marie Aflak keep the bakery going today, continuing the legacy Marie's parents built. It is still a shop where regulars are greeted by name and the pita is never more than a few hours old.

Where we come from

Almost fifty years on 3rd Avenue

A short history of a shop that has never needed to change much to keep people coming back.

1976

Antoine and Frangie Tabet open Mid-East Bakery and Grocery on 3rd Avenue.

1990s

Daughter Marie takes on a larger role in the shop, learning the ovens and the regulars.

2000s

Marie and her husband Tony Aflak take over daily operations of the bakery.

Today

Sons Alex and Thomas work alongside their parents, the third generation behind the counter.
