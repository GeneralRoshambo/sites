A North Scranton Summer Tradition

Main Ave Ice Cream has been the walk-up stop for the North Scranton neighborhood, scooping hard and soft serve from a window right on North Main Avenue. No dining room, no drive-thru, just a counter, a menu board and a line that forms most evenings once the weather turns warm.

Stop in for a cone on the way home, or bring the whole street over for sundaes after a Little League game up the road.

What's at the Window

Cones & Cups

Hand-dipped hard ice cream, soft serve twist, and kiddie sizes for the little ones.

Sundaes

Hot fudge, caramel, and seasonal toppings built to order at the window.

Shakes & Floats

Hand-spun milkshakes and root beer floats made fresh, no machine shortcuts.
