The Story Behind Hollywood Painting and Design

A painting company built on a film-industry standard of flawless surfaces.

Owner & founder

Jason Oertling

Hollywood Painting and Design is led by Jason Oertling, a licensed commercial and residential painter who spent years in the film industry. On a set, every surface has to read as perfect under bright, unforgiving light, and that habit of getting the details right carried straight into the company.

When the film work slowed, Jason turned that craft into a painting business for the Slidell area. The name is a nod to where he came from, and the work reflects it. Clean lines, careful prep, and specialty finishes that most painting crews never learn to do.

Whether it is a full home repaint, a storefront, or a single accent wall in Venetian plaster, the goal is a finish you would be proud to show off.
