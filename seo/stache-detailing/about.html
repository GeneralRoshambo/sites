The Story Behind Stache Detailing

A local, hands-on detailing service built on doing the small things right.

Owner & operator

Caleb Hautala

Stache Detailing is run by Caleb Hautala, who turned a real passion for clean, well-kept vehicles into a mobile detailing business serving the Brainerd Lakes area. Every detail is done personally, by hand, with an eye for the parts that get overlooked.

The idea is simple. Bring a genuinely thorough detail to people where they already are, in their own driveway, without the rushed feeling of a drive-through wash. That means real time spent on the interior, careful attention on the paint, and honest advice about what your vehicle actually needs.

Because Stache is local to Deerwood and the surrounding lakes towns, you are working with someone from the community, not a chain. That shows up in the finish and in how we treat your vehicle.
