A Century on Birney Avenue

Joyce's Cafe has held the same corner in Minooka since 1918, four generations of the same neighborhood walking in for a burger, a beer and whatever game is on. It is the proud home of the Minooka Notre Dame Club, and the kind of bar where the bartender already knows your order.

Pull up a stool, order off the grill, and settle in with the rest of the block.
